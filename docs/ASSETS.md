# Festival görselleri

Bu görseller, kurgusal Northline Festival demosu için yerleşik image generation aracıyla üretildi. Gerçek bir etkinliğin belgesel fotoğrafı veya gerçekleşmiş bir organizasyonun referansı olarak sunulmamalıdır. Görseller yerel dosya olarak paketlenir; üçüncü taraf görsel sunucusuna istek gönderilmez.

## Konser atmosferi

Dosya: `apps/web/public/images/festival-evening.png`

Kullanım: Web genel bakışındaki festival tanıtımı. Geniş sahne fotoğrafı, metnin görüntüyü kapatmaması için sağ ağırlıklı kompozisyona sahiptir.

Üretim istemi:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one wide landscape editorial photograph for a premium festival operations website, at least 1536px wide. A real open-air contemporary music festival in an urban park at blue hour: view from side of the mixing desk towards a large black steel concert stage on the right, amber-white stage spotlights, understated red accent lighting, a densely packed but realistic crowd of adult music fans in foreground, trees framing background, subtle stage haze, deep charcoal sky, analog documentary photography, realistic 35mm lens, restrained cinematic color, authentic concert production equipment and trusses. Strong detail and texture, naturally imperfect, no CGI or illustration. Left side somewhat darker to support white title overlay; stage on right third. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no readable signs, no extreme neon pink or purple, no confetti. This is an atmospheric website banner photograph, not a UI mockup.

## Sahne arkası

Dosyalar: `apps/web/public/images/festival-backstage.png`, `apps/mobile/assets/festival-backstage.png`

Kullanım: Web giriş ekranı ve mobil festival kartı. Mobilde fotoğraf dar kadraja uyarlanır; görsel başlık, program ekranına açılan bir düğmedir.

Üretim istemi:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a vertical editorial photograph, portrait 2:3 composition, for the login cover of a professional outdoor festival production management app. Candid documentary view from backstage at a large open-air concert in a city park at dusk. In foreground on the left, one adult sound technician in simple charcoal workwear and a discreet crew lanyard seen from behind / three-quarter back, working at a real mixing console with tactile faders, dark road cases and carefully routed audio cables. Beyond him a black steel truss stage and amber spotlights illuminate a distant crowd, natural haze, deep warm browns and neutral charcoal tones, honest analog grain, realistic photography with 50mm lens, natural imperfections, no glamorous pose, no readable labels, no text, no logo or watermark, no neon purple or cyan. Leave upper half and left foreground relatively dark for white editorial text overlay. Not an illustration, not a mockup.
